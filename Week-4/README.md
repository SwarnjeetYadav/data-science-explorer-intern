# Week 4 — Comprehensive Data Science Report and Insights Presentation Plan

## Project
**Customer Churn Analytics — Insights, Recommendations & Presentation Strategy**

## Objective
Develop a polished, executive-level plan for communicating data science findings from a hypothetical customer churn project to technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## What was completed
- Executive summary and business context
- Methodology overview covering data preparation, EDA, feature engineering, modeling, evaluation, insight synthesis, and action design
- Hypothetical insights clearly labeled as illustrative because no real dataset was provided
- Mock-up visualizations for churn distribution, tenure/churn relationship, engagement risk, and executive trends
- Visualization selection principles and chart rationale
- Translation of analytical signals into actionable business insights
- Recommendations connected to measurable business outcomes
- Executive storytelling flow from business question → evidence → finding → impact → action → measurement
- Suggested 8-slide executive presentation structure
- Future research and validation recommendations
- Limitations and responsible communication guidance
- 33-hour Week 4 workload plan
- Evaluation criteria for clarity, visualization quality, actionable insights, and stakeholder communication

## Important note
All numerical values and charts in the Week 4 report are **mock-up/illustrative examples**. They are not results from a real dataset. The report is a planning and communication exercise as required by the Week 4 assignment.

## Deliverable
The accompanying DOCX report contains the complete executive report and presentation plan, including integrated visual aids, methodology, hypothetical insights, recommendations, communication strategy, limitations, and timeline.
