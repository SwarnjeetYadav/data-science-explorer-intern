# Data Science Explorer Internship

This repository contains my work for the **Virtual Data Science Explorer Internship**.

## Week 1 — Data Science Project Planning and Strategy Design

### Project
**Customer Churn Prediction & Retention Analytics**

### Objective
Design a complete Python-based data science project plan for identifying customers who may be at higher risk of churn and understanding the factors associated with churn.

### Week 1 Focus
This week focuses on **conceptualization, planning, methodology, technical architecture, timeline, tools, expected outcomes, and risk management**. No dataset or model implementation is required at this stage.
